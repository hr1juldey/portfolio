one specimen — shipped, public, live

HistoGate stain-invariant tissue classifier

A 9-class colorectal H&E classifier that declines tissue it doesn't recognize instead of guessing. First version scored 97% on its own data and 78% on a hospital it never saw — the fix was Beer–Lambert staining physics baked into training, a k-NN familiarity gate, conformal sets on top. Served live from my own GPU. The three failed training runs are in the repo.
