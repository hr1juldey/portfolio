HistoGate

Stain-invariant 9-class colorectal H&E classifier. First version scored 97% on its own data and 78% on a hospital it never saw — fixed with Beer–Lambert staining physics in training, a k-NN familiarity gate, conformal sets on top. It declines tissue it doesn't recognize. The three failed training runs are in the repo.
